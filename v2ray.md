## Subscription maintenance

The aggregator accepts plain-text or base64 URI lists and produces a deduplicated base64 subscription. Unsupported formats are skipped. Parsing does not verify connectivity or full protocol compatibility. See [maintenance instructions](README.md#maintaining-subscriptions) for local commands, update behavior, and troubleshooting. The client table is a legacy reference; confirm maintenance status and download origin before installation.
